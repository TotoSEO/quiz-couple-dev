### A2. Royalties and two worked examples

Sources: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834330 (paperback), G77F3WPD3KQLJTFS (hardcover), G8BKPU9AGVZSF9QF (print pricing terms), GPQL5W3J6WNRCZTV (EU taxes).

- Amazon channel: royalty = (50% or 60%) × list price − printing cost. The 60% rate applies at or above **9.99 USD / 9.99 EUR / 7.99 GBP**; 50% applies at or below 9.98.
- Expanded Distribution (paperback only): 40% × list price − printing cost.
- **The list price you enter for EU and UK marketplaces excludes VAT.** Amazon adds VAT on top, and the royalty is computed on the VAT-exclusive price. KDP's VAT table: France 5.5% reduced / 20% standard; Germany 7% / 19%; Italy 4% / 22%; Spain 4% / 21%; Netherlands 9% / 21%; UK 0% / 20%; Belgium 6% / 21%; Ireland 0% / 23%.
- KDP warns that "**notebooks, planners, diaries, coloring books, activity books**" may be charged the **standard** VAT rate. The customer pays more; the royalty does not change.
- Minimum list price = printing cost ÷ royalty rate. Maximum: 250 USD / EUR / GBP.

**Example 1: 120 pages, black ink, 6x9 (regular), list price 12.99**

| | Amazon.com | Amazon.fr / .de (EUR) | Amazon.co.uk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Printing cost | 1.00 + 120×0.012 = **$2.44** | 0.75 + 120×0.012 = **€2.19** | 0.85 + 120×0.010 = **£2.05** |
| Royalty at 60% (12.99 = list price excl. VAT) | 7.79 − 2.44 = **$5.35** | 7.79 − 2.19 = **€5.60** | **£5.74** |
| Groundwood instead | $5.43 | €5.68 | – |
| Expanded Distribution (40%) | $2.76, but journals, activity, puzzle and trivia books are refused by the distributors | n/a | |
| Customer price if 12.99 is the list price | $12.99 + sales tax | FR €13.70 (5.5%) or €15.59 (20%) | £12.99 (book) / £15.59 (journal) |
| If **€12.99 is the price paid by the customer** in France | | list price €12.31 → royalty **€5.20** (5.5%), or list price €10.83 → **€4.31** (20%) | |

Minimum list price: $4.88 / €4.38 (at the 50% rate).
For comparison, the same book at $8.99 earns **$2.06** (50% rate), and at $9.99 earns **$3.55**.

**Example 2: 100 pages, premium color, 8.5x8.5 (large), list price 19.99**

| | Amazon.com | Amazon.fr / .de (EUR) | Amazon.co.uk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Printing cost | 1.00 + 100×0.08 = **$9.00** | 0.75 + 100×0.0715 = **€7.90** | 0.85 + 100×0.0598 = **£6.83** |
| Royalty at 60% (19.99 excl. VAT) | 11.99 − 9.00 = **$2.99** | 11.99 − 7.90 = **€4.09** | **£5.16** |
| If €19.99 is the price paid by the customer in France | | royalty **€3.47** (5.5%) or **€2.10** (20%) | |
| Minimum list price | $15.00 | €13.17 | £11.38 |
| Expanded Distribution | 8.5x8.5 premium color is eligible, but at $19.99 the royalty is −$1.00 (minimum list price $22.50) | | |

Same 100 pages in **standard color**, 8.5x8.5: printing cost $5.02 / €4.25 / £3.55, royalty at 19.99 = **$6.97 / €7.74 / £8.44**. Standard color is lower quality: inkjet on light paper. Premium color would cost $10.60 on a 120-page large-trim book (official example).

Hardcover for reference: 6x9, black, 120 pages = $7.09 / €6.09 / £5.35. 8.25x11, premium color, 100 pages = $13.65 / €11.85 / £10.15. There is no square hardcover.

### A3. Formats, paper, bleed, finish, limits

Source: Set Trim Size, Bleed, and Margins, https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GVBQ3CMEQW3W2VL6

**Paperback trims and page limits** (B/W white – cream – groundwood – standard color – premium color):

| Trim | Pages |
|---|---|
| 5x8, 5.06x7.81, 5.25x8, 5.5x8.5, 6x9 (regular) | 24–828 / 24–776 / 24–812 / 72–600 / 24–828 |
| 6.14x9.21, 6.69x9.61, 7x10, 7.44x9.69, 7.5x9.25, 8x10 (large) | same |
| 8.25x6 and **8.25x8.25** | 24–800 / 24–750 / 24–784 / 72–600 / 24–800 |
| **8.5x8.5** and 8.5x11 | 24–590 / 24–550 / 24–578 / 72–600 / 24–590 |
| 8.27x11.69 (A4) | 24–780 / 24–730 / 24–764 / not available / 24–590 |

- Custom paperback trims: width 4–8.5 in, height 6–11.69 in.
- **Hardcover**: only 5.5x8.5, 6x9 (regular), 6.14x9.21, 7x10, 8.25x11 (large), 75–550 pages, black or premium color. No square hardcover, no standard color, no groundwood.

**Paper**
- Black ink: white or cream, 50–61 lb (74–90 GSM).
- Groundwood: 45 lb (60 GSM).
- Standard color: 50–61 lb (74–90 GSM), inkjet, matte.
- Premium color: 60–71 lb (88–105 GSM), white.
- Cover: 80 lb (220 GSM), **glossy or matte**.
- Hardcover: case laminate on 2 mm board with glossy or matte lamination. Endsheets are white or cream. Black-and-white headband above 120 pages.
- Spine text only on books with more than 79 pages.

**Bleed and margins**
- Bleed: 0.125 in (3.2 mm) on the top, bottom and outside edges. Page size = trim + 0.125 in in width and + 0.25 in in height. Example: 6x9 becomes 6.125 x 9.25.
- Outside margins: at least 0.25 in without bleed, 0.375 in with bleed.
- Gutter: 0.375 in for 24–150 pages, 0.5 in for 151–300 pages, and wider beyond.

**What KDP cannot do**
- KDP offers only perfect-bound paperbacks and case-laminate hardcovers. The hardcover page says: "Dust jackets, cloth bound, and other hardcover formats aren't available."
- No KDP page offers spiral or coil binding, lay-flat binding, perforation, ribbons, stickers, scratch-off, inserts, envelopes or tabs. This is an inference from the official option lists, not an explicit statement.
- Interior and paper type are locked after publication; switching between white, cream and groundwood stays possible for black-ink books.
- Standard color is not available in A4.
- Color availability by marketplace is inconsistent across KDP pages. The color ink page lists .com, .ca, .co.jp, .co.uk and .es/.it/.fr/.de/.ie/.com.be (plus .com.au for premium), not .nl, .pl or .se, while the cost page has euro, PLN and SEK color rates. Check NL before relying on it.

### A4. Where KDP prints and sells, delivery, author copies

- **Paperback sold on**: .com, .ca, .co.jp, .com.au, .co.uk, .de, .es, .fr, .it, .nl, .pl, .se, .ie, .com.be. **Hardcover**: the same list without .co.jp; .ca only through .com. (https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834280)
- **"amazon.com.br, amazon.com.in, and amazon.com.mx do not support paperback and hardcover distribution."** No KDP print offer exists on any Latin American Amazon store. Author copies for Brazil are ordered on Amazon.com. Whether .com ships these books to Mexico or Brazil was not verified.
- **Publishing delay**: about 3 business days, but "for low-content books, such as journals or notebooks, reviews may take **3–4 weeks**" (https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G202173620).
- **Payment**: about 60 days after the end of the month. Expanded Distribution is paid about 60 days after the distributor reports.
- **Author and proof copies**:
  - Price = printing cost for the chosen marketplace × quantity, plus shipping and taxes at checkout ("lowest price we can offer, no additional discounts").
  - Up to 5 proofs and up to 999 author copies per order.
  - For France, order on Amazon.fr; Portugal uses .es, .fr or .it; Belgium, the Netherlands, Ireland, Sweden and Poland use .de, .es, .fr or .it.
  - Shipping: "We're unable to provide a direct formula for shipping costs". Copies are not eligible for free or Prime shipping. Customs fees may apply if shipped from outside the EU. A shipping cost for France was not found.
  - Delivery date is shown at checkout; manufacturing time "fluctuates according to total order volume".
  - Pages: GVEG4YA9G2T7N6DR, G2MYNEKHT443C2H2, GG6GRS7TKXVG6AGW, GL9S534PE857E2CN.

### A5. Low-content books, AI disclosure, quality rules

**Low-content** (https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GGE5T76TWKA85DJM)
- Definition: "minimal or no content on the interior pages… repetitive, and designed to be filled in by the user".
- Examples given: notebooks, planners, **diaries/journals, prompt journals**, log books, **coupon books**.
- **Not** low-content: puzzle books, coloring books, activity books, and other books whose pages are not repetitive.
- Rules:
  - No ISBN required: publish without an ISBN or with your own. The free KDP ISBN is **not** allowed.
  - **No Look Inside** without an ISBN (use A+ Content images instead).
  - **No Expanded Distribution**, whatever the ISBN option.
  - No series and no release-date scheduling.
  - The ISBN option cannot be changed after publishing (unpublish and republish).
- A couples quiz or game book with real questions is not low-content. A fill-in prompt journal is.

**Expanded Distribution exclusions** (https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GQTT4W3T5AYK7L45)
- Refused: journals, notebooks, planners, calendars, agendas, coloring books, and topics such as activity books, puzzle books, trivia books, workbooks, joke books.
- ED is not available for hardcovers.
- 8.25x8.25 is never ED-eligible. 8.5x8.5 is ED-eligible only in color.

**AI** (Content Guidelines, https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200672390)
- "We require you to inform us of AI-generated content (text, images, or translations)…", including cover and interior images, even after substantial edits.
- AI-assisted content (you created it, AI edited or brainstormed) does not need disclosure.
- The publisher remains responsible for IP compliance.

**Quality**
- KDP "do[es] not allow content that's typically disappointing to customers" and misleading descriptions. It may recategorize books or remove titles (an appeal is possible).
- Daily cap of **3 new titles per day**, announced in September 2023 and confirmed to the Authors Guild (https://authorsguild.org/news/amazon-adds-to-kdp-generative-ai-policy-caps-daily-self-publishing-uploads/ ; https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/digital/content-and-e-books/article/93207-kdp-will-limit-daily-number-of-new-titles.html). Current status not re-checked.

### A6. Amazon Associates commission on your own KDP book

- In the US Operating Agreement / Commission Income Statement (https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies) and the French "Politiques du Programme" (https://partenaires.amazon.fr/help/operating/policies), **no clause excludes products that the Associate publishes or sells**. The excluded products are listed in section 3: products sold off Amazon, alcohol for licensed businesses, prescription drugs, and products Amazon flags.
- What is prohibited (policy u): "You will not directly or indirectly purchase any Product(s)… through Special Links… and you will not permit, request or encourage any of your friends, relatives, employees, contractors, or business relations to… purchase…". Purchases "for resale or commercial use" are also prohibited.
- So linking the site's readers to the KDP book with the site's tag can earn the commission **on top of** the royalty, as long as no self, friend or family buying and no buying author copies through the tag.
- Clauses 2(c)–(d) disqualify purchases referred by ads you buy on keywords containing "amazon" or "kindle", and by any link displayed on a search engine or its sponsored-ads network: the tag must sit on the site itself. Qualifying revenue excludes VAT and shipping.
- The disclosure "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases" is required.
- The commission rate for books per marketplace is behind the Associates login: not verified.

## B. Alternatives and complements

### B1. Lulu (incl. Lulu Print API / Lulu Direct)

- **Prints in France**: "We produce books at our print facilities across the world, including locations in **France**, India, Australia, the United Kingdom, Canada, and the United States." Orders go to the printer nearest the destination; you cannot choose the printer.
  - Production time: 3–5 business days.
  - Sources: https://help.lulu.com/en/support/solutions/articles/64000255307-shipping-the-basics ; https://www.lulu.com/sell/sell-on-your-site/print-api
- **Formats**: coil-bound, paperback, hardcover (casewrap, linen, dust jacket), magazines, photo books, calendars, notebooks; "3,000+ configurations".
  - Papers listed in the calculator: 60# cream or white uncoated, and 60/70/80/100# white coated. Lulu recommends coated paper with premium color for photos. Standard color is wet inkjet, and Lulu warns against image-heavy use.
- **API and integrations**: REST API with webhooks; Shopify, Wix and WooCommerce integrations. No API or monthly fee is mentioned on the API page.
- **Prices** (Lulu blog, undated, USD, 100 pages, cheapest paper, black ink; https://blog.lulu.com/print-on-demand-costs-for-authors/):

### B11. France: legal and tax basics (short)

- **VAT**
  - Books 5.5% in France, standard rate 20%.
  - KDP's EU tax page warns that notebooks, planners, diaries, coloring and activity books may be taxed at the standard rate. A fill-in journal is therefore likely at 20%; a quiz or game book with editorial content is more likely a "livre" at 5.5%. Confirm with the BOFiP definition of "livre" (not fetched) or a tax ruling (rescrit).
- **Franchise en base de TVA** (micro): for 2026 the thresholds are unchanged at **€85,000** for sales of goods (€93,500 tolerance) and €37,500 for services (€41,250 tolerance). The planned single €25,000 threshold was abandoned (https://entreprendre.service-public.fr/vosdroits/F21746, checked 1 Jan 2026).
- **Other EU countries**: a **€10,000** EU-wide threshold applies to B2C distance sales. Below it, French VAT rules apply; above it, the VAT of the buyer's country is due, declared through the **OSS** Union scheme (quarterly) (https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/taxation/vat/cross-border-vat/index_en.htm, checked 13 Jul 2026; https://vat-one-stop-shop.ec.europa.eu/one-stop-shop_en).
- **Dépôt légal (BnF)**
  - Mandatory for books, including self-published and print-on-demand titles. Send **1 printed copy** with a declaration; there is no minimum print run, and the mention "impression à la demande" can replace the print date.
  - **Activity books are covered**, but documents to be filled in by the user, treated as stationery, are **excluded**: "cahiers d'écriture, livres de raison, agendas, organiseurs, carnets, journaux intimes".
  - Sources: https://www.bnf.fr/fr/le-depot-legal-de-lautoedition-la-bnf ; https://www.bnf.fr/fr/centre-d-aide/depot-legal-editeur-mode-demploi
- **ISBN via AFNIL is no longer free.** A first request costs **€37 excl. VAT** (3 weeks), €87 (1 week) or €154 (2 working days); additional lists from €28; VAT 20% (https://www.afnil.org/tarification/). The free KDP ISBN only works on KDP and is not allowed for low-content books.
- **Loi Lang** (loi 81-766, art. 1, consolidated version read 2026-11-07, https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000000517179)
  - The publisher sets one public price. Retailers must sell at **95–100%** of it. Free delivery is forbidden; a **minimum delivery charge is set by arrêté**. The commonly cited €3 below €35 of books, since October 2023, was not re-verified here.
  - KDP lists France among the fixed-price-law countries for paperbacks and hardcovers: "you must set the same list price for any retailer… notifying the designated authorities where applicable" (https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200641280).
  - Consequence: the VAT-inclusive Amazon.fr price must equal the price on the owner's own site (maximum 5% discount), and direct sales of a "livre" must charge the minimum delivery fee. A pure fill-in journal (stationery) is probably outside the law, to be confirmed.
- **Packaging EPR**: anyone who puts packaging on the French market must join an eco-organisation such as CITEO and declare volumes. Amazon.fr requires the seller's UIN (Gelato article above). EU GPSR product-safety rules also apply to consumer products; Gelato and Lulu both publish GPSR guidance.
