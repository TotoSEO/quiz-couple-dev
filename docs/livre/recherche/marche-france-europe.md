# Interactive couple books & couple products: France first, then Germany, Spain, Italy

For: quiz-couple.com (own-brand interactive couple book, print on demand, sold from the site).
Research date: 2026-11-07. Every number below is a snapshot read on that date, with its source.

## 0. How the figures were obtained (read before using any number)

- amazon.fr/.de/.it/.es answer 503 to direct fetches; Fnac, Cultura, Decitre, Thalia, Action and
  camelcamelcamel answer 403. I did not try to get around these blocks.
- Amazon star ratings and rating counts therefore come from search-engine snapshots of Amazon pages
  (or of pages that quote them). They can be weeks or months old; when two snapshots disagree, both are given.
- Directly fetched (live on 2026-11-07): editions-jouvence.com, hachette.fr, editions365.eu, livreshebdo.fr,
  eu.theadventurechallenge.com, vertellis.fr, vertellis.de, dimoijeux.com, simonjan.de, cocoonetoncouple.com,
  danslemanoirauxlivres.fr, buzzjeux.fr, lemeilleuravis.fr, kita.de, mintkind.com, marabout.com.
- The shared web-search budget (200 searches per turn, all agents together) ran out before the Italian and

## 3. What makes the French leaders sell

1. The single French book with real volume is 19 years old: "Tout sur nous" (pocket, 9,90 €, ~870 Amazon
   ratings + 83 Fnac). Promise: write your love story together, from the first meeting to "les sujets qui fâchent".
   Distribution in every bookshop (Livre de Poche) matters as much as the concept.
2. Card games beat books by ~5x in reviews (Osmooz ~4 600 vs best book ~870). French couples buy "jouer ce soir,
   sans écrire": reviewers of books complain "long à remplir", "pas assez de place pour écrire"; blogs recommend a
   challenge book or Osmooz "for couples who don't like writing".
3. Gift positioning is explicit: KDP subtitles stack "idée cadeau original ... anniversaire, mariage,
   Saint-Valentin"; publishers sell the object: gold foil "effet cadeau garanti" (Éditions 365), hardcover
   (Hachette Pratique 14,90 €), stickers, "carnet" look. Key windows: Saint-Valentin, Noël, anniversaire, mariage/EVJF.
4. Price ladder: workbooks 5,70-8,95 € (Jouvence) < pocket 9,90 € < carnets 9,90-14,90 € < card games 12-20 € <
   premium imports 29,99-49,95 € < experience boxes 80-160 €.
5. "Coquin" is a full sub-segment in France (Jouvence "câlins coquins", Miss Suzy, Osmooz Hot, Carta Sutra,
   Orgazmo, "Oseras-tu", "69 défis hot", Marabout's "Dévanillez votre couple").
6. The US "What I Love About You" mechanic did not stick in France (Marabout's 2019 version is out of print),
   while Germany turned it into a franchise (riva, below).

## 4. Typical interior structures

- "Notre histoire" memory book (Ribeiro, Guérin, Bouvet, Pellegrini): portrait of each partner from both points of
  view, the first meeting, firsts, trips, milestones, photo pages, dreams/future; KDP versions add "portrait
  chinois", small quizzes and "bons à offrir". 70-100 p (KDP), 96 p (Marabout, riva), 220-290 p (Éditions 365, Poche).
- Quiz / questions carnet (Éditions 365 "Toi & Moi", Atout Cœur): double pages "toi / moi", themed questions
  (qualities, habits, childhood, family, friends, work), quizzes with points and a forfeit for the loser.
- Challenge / bucket list (100-132 challenges; Couple Challenge®, Darlena): one challenge per page with date,
  comment and a photo slot (Polaroid/Instax), sometimes time and budget per date; premium versions hide each
  challenge under a scratch-off layer.
- Activity workbook (Alexandre): 10 ice-breakers, 10 "get to know each other", 10 fun activities, 10 intimate
  games + a forfeit box. Jouvence workbooks: short tests, exercises, illustrations, 64 p.
- Recurrent complaints to design against: no room to write, repeated questions and errors (Bouvet), "childish /
  superficial" interior and thin cover (Guérin), "easy and simple" activities (Alexandre 3,8/5), questions that get
  boring for couples who already know each other (Jokubo), "redundant and expensive" (Social Couples), "can create
  tensions" (Ribeiro).

## 5. France vs US (what the data supports)

- Supply, not demand, is the French gap in quizzes: US quiz/question books sold on Amazon.fr carry 928-1 557
  ratings, French-language ones 1-20.
- US premium formats travel at US prices (Adventure Challenge 29,99 €, WNRS 29-50 €); French-made books sit at
  9,90-14,90 € and French-made games at 12-20 €.
- Titles use "toi" to the partner ("Toi & Moi", "Ce que j'aime chez toi") and "vous" for the couple ("le livre de
  votre histoire d'amour"). Humour and a frank "coquin" register are mainstream (Juduku/ATM Gaming, Benrik).
- French retail (Fnac, Cultura, Nature & Découvertes) stocks traditional gift books and French card games; the KDP
  books live on Amazon only.

## 8. Gaps and opportunities for a quiz-couple.com book

1. Own the French "livre de quiz de couple": demand is proven (US quiz books 900-1 500 ratings on Amazon.fr,
   French quiz card games: Osmooz ~4 600 ratings, DIMOI ~2 000 by its own count, "Qui Sait Mieux Qui" first on one affiliate list), French
   supply is weak (KDP quiz books with 1-20 ratings, a 2024-2026 wave of "Duel / Battle / Toi vs Moi" books
   with no visible traction yet). No publisher or site brand owns it.
2. Design against the known complaints: play more than write (tick boxes, scores, verdicts like the site's tests,
   two colours for her and him or player 1 and 2 that match the pink and purple mascots), enough room, replayable
   duels, an adult tone with cute characters (Guérin's "childish" reviews show the risk), and a
   "coquin" chapter or edition (big French sub-segment, and the site already has a quiz coquin).
3. Link paper and site: a QR code per quiz to play it online or remotely, compare scores, unlock a bonus; no
   competitor has a live quiz engine and 150 000 visits a month behind its book.
4. Price where French buyers already buy: 9,90-14,90 € for a book (12,90-14,90 € in colour), under the 15-20 € of
   Osmooz/DIMOI and far under the 29,99 € imports; a "notre histoire" mascot carnet is the classic gift (Saint-
   Valentin, Noël, anniversaires, mariages).
5. Series potential, as riva did in Germany: one concept, several editions (quiz, coquin, "notre histoire",
   occasions). For Germany, the format that sells is the 20-30 € scratch / challenge date book; for Spain, localized
   premium formats; Italy needs a proper scan before any translation.
