# Interactive books for couples: the English-language market (research notes)

Collected 2026-11-07 for quiz-couple.com (plan: a print-on-demand interactive book for couples, promoted on the site).

## 0. Method and caveats (read first)

- **Amazon blocked every direct fetch** (amazon.com / amazon.co.uk answered 503; camelcamelcamel 403). I did not try to get around the bot check.
  Amazon figures below therefore come from **search-engine snapshots of Amazon listings** in several marketplaces
  (.com, .co.uk, .ca, .com.au, .in, .de, .se, .sg, .nl, .jp...). For the same ASIN/ISBN Amazon shows a **pooled "global ratings" count**,
  which is why the .in / .de / .com.au counts of one product are close to each other. **Snapshot dates are unknown** (indexed copies, seen on 2026-11-07),
  so treat every Amazon count as "order of magnitude, 2025-2026".
- **Sales estimates**: ASInsight (asinsight.com) publishes Amazon US keyword reports with **estimated monthly unit sales** (third-party models, dated March-July 2026).
  They cover card games and scrapbooks well, books poorly.
- **TikTok Shop** keyword pages were fetched live on **2026-11-07** ("sold" = cumulative units shown on the listing).
- **Brand/publisher pages** fetched 2026-11-07 (self-reported claims are labelled as such).
- **KDP** cost/royalty formulas from the official KDP help pages (fetched 2026-11-07).
- The session's shared web-search quota ran out near the end: a few families (couple gratitude journals, relationship "year in pixels", long-distance-specific books, Christian devotionals) were **not researched in depth** and are marked "not found".
- Nothing below is invented: when a figure was not found it says **not found**.

**Corrections to the brief**
- "Questions for Couples" (469 conversation starters) is by **Marcus & Ashley Kusi** (Our Peaceful Family, 2017), not J.R. James.
  **J.R. James** is a separate self-published author of spicy couple game books (Would You Rather..., Truth or Dare, Never Have I Ever, Pillow Talk Trivia, The Naughty Newlywed Game, Hot or Not Quiz for Couples; imprint "Love & Desire Press"); no rating counts found.
- No Amazon product called "Scratch Adventure" by Gift Republic was found. Gift Republic's couples product is
  *Scratch Off Book | Couples Edition | 100 Scratch Off Date Ideas* and it is small (15-19 ratings). The scratch-off leader is **The Adventure Challenge**.

## 4. Why the leaders sell

1. **They are gifts first.** The buyer is usually one partner (or a friend) buying for Valentine's Day, an anniversary, Christmas, an engagement, a wedding or a shower; listings stuff the subtitle with occasions and recipients (e.g. Adventure Challenge on Amazon: "Couples Gift for Mother's Day, Anniversary or Wedding Gift"; Moore book marketed for Valentine's/anniversary; Promptly for weddings, anniversaries, Valentine's; reviewers buy "one for every shower gift"). Small, giftable trims (Knock Knock 4.5 x 3.25 in, Compendium 8 x 7.5 in hardcover) and **impulse prices** ($10-17) drive the biggest rating counts.
2. **Low effort, high emotion.** Fill-in books take one sitting and produce a unique, personal object (Knock Knock, I Wrote a Book About You: 64 pages). Daily journals ask for "one or two sentences" (Q&A a Day), BestSelf "as little as one hour per week", Promptly "~20 minutes per week".
3. **A mechanic an app cannot replicate.** Scratch-off "mystery" + "no takebacks" (Adventure Challenge), time capsule over 3 years (Q&A a Day: you see how your answers changed), secret answers revealed together (Are We Compatible's divider, two-journal sets). The physical reveal is the product.
4. **Experience + keepsake in one object.** Adventure Challenge = 50 dates + photo/journal space; scrapbooks sell blank; keepsake value justifies $25-50.
5. **Social proof moat.** The leaders date from 2012-2019 and sit on 3K-22K ratings; the 2025-2026 KDP clones ("Would You Rather for couples", "Our Q&A a Day" look-alikes at BSR ~800K) barely register. Copying a title does not copy demand.
6. **Brands, lines and refreshes.** Knock Knock "Fill in the Love" (You, Us, Mom, Sis...), Compendium (You, Us, Mom, Dad, Friend, Gold edition 2025), Adventure Challenge (Couples, Vol. 2, In Bed, Quickies, Family, Friends, Solo, Kids), Q&A a Day (Hearts edition Dec 2025), One Question a Day (one-year edition Jan 2026), Callisto's "Relationship Books for Couples", Kusi's 8-9 book series. Each new edition reuses the audience.
7. **Content-native + DTC.** The Adventure Challenge (DTC on Shopify, $80M sales in 2021 per ShipBob, 25% international) and WNRS/BestSelf/Relatable are social-first brands; card games and scratch-offs film well (reactions, unboxing). TikTok Shop is dominated by them, not by books.
8. **Credibility for the "serious" segment.** Gottman (*Eight Dates* 5.3K, *Seven Principles* 15K), Esther Perel's game, "psychology-backed"/"therapy-based" claims (BestSelf, Promptly, Relatable).

## 5. Typical interior structures (from publisher/product descriptions)

- **Daily multi-year journal** (Q&A a Day, One Question a Day): intro/how-to page; 365 dated pages; one question at the top; per year a small block split for **Partner 1 / Partner 2** (1-2 sentences each); question mix: about the relationship, about each other, individual.
- **Weekly journal** (Promptly, Callisto 52-week, BestSelf 13-week): intro + how to use; 52 (or 13) weekly spreads; **duplicate pages so each partner answers alone**; 3 questions/week; weekly connection tip or date idea; periodic check-ins (BestSelf: weekly + monthly), extra sections (Date Ideas, Goals and Dreams, Our Love Story, Snapshot).
- **Fill-in gift book** (Knock Knock, Compendium, Peter Pauper): To/From page; ~50 sentence starters, one per page, illustrated (Compendium) or minimal (Knock Knock); playful to tender tone.
- **Scratch-off adventure book** (Adventure Challenge, Let's Roam): ground rules ("no takebacks"), icon key (cost tier, time of day, duration, location, weather), themed sections, 50 scratch boxes, after each a page with photo space + journaling lines; extras (postcards, bonding questions).
- **Question book** (Kusi, Moore, Book of Questions): short intro (how to use on dates/road trips), chapters by theme (relationship, past, future, life, hypotheticals), numbered questions, sometimes rules to score points; usually no writing space.
- **Activity book** (LoveBook, Rockridge): mixed games: quizzes, fill-in-the-blanks, word scrambles, puzzles, date-night plans, bucket lists.
- **Quiz book** (Summersdale): series of themed quizzes (likes/dislikes, history, goals); score/compare; adult section.
- **Keepsake** (Book of Us, Chartwell, Kusi Our Love Story): chronological chapters (dating, engagement & wedding, marriage), photo spaces, timeline.

## 7. KDP economics (official KDP help pages, fetched 2026-11-07)

- Paperback royalty: **60% of list price at or above $9.99 (US) / £7.99 (UK)**, 50% below; Expanded Distribution 40%; minus printing cost.
- Printing cost, Amazon.com: black ink 24-110 p. = $2.30 flat (regular trim); 110-828 p. = $1.00 + $0.012/page; standard colour (72-600 p.) $1.00 + $0.0255/page (large trim $0.0402); premium colour $1.00 + $0.065/page (large $0.08). Large trim = wider than 6.12 in or taller than 9 in.
- Hardcover (case laminate), Amazon.com: black ink 75-108 p. $6.80 flat; 110-550 p. $5.65 + $0.012/page; premium colour $5.65 + $0.065/page (large $0.08). Amazon.co.uk equivalents in GBP on the same pages (e.g. paperback B&W £0.85 + £0.010/page).
- Worked examples (my arithmetic from those formulas):
  - 6 x 9 in B&W paperback, 120 p., $12.99: print $2.44, royalty **$5.35**.
  - 6 x 9 in B&W paperback, 368 p. (3-year daily journal), $16.99: print $5.42, royalty **$4.78**.
  - 8.5 x 8.5 in (large trim) standard-colour paperback, 120 p., $19.99: print $5.82, royalty **$6.17**.
  - 6 x 9 in premium-colour paperback, 120 p., $19.99: print $8.80, royalty $3.19.
  - Hardcover examples assume the same 60% rate (the royalty page fetched states the 50/60% rule for paperbacks only):
    6 x 9 in B&W hardcover, 200 p., $24.99: print $8.05, royalty $6.94;
    6 x 9 in premium-colour hardcover, 120 p., $24.99: print $13.45, royalty $1.54 (colour hardcover barely works on KDP).
  - UK 6 x 9 in B&W paperback, 120 p., £9.99: print £2.05, royalty £3.94.
- What KDP cannot do (format limits of perfect-bound paperback and case-laminate hardcover): scratch-off panels, spiral/flexi binding, foil, ribbon, clear jackets, pockets, perforated tear-outs. The mechanics that make the Adventure Challenge or Knock Knock distinctive need offset printing (and stock, e.g. FBA) or another printer.
- Kindlepreneur's rule of thumb for print BSR: under 10,000 = strong daily sales; under 100,000 = consistent movement; above 500,000 = infrequent sales ([Kindlepreneur](https://kindlepreneur.com/amazon-kdp-sales-rank-calculator/)). Source pages: [printing costs](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834340), [royalties](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834330), [hardcover costs](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GHT976ZKSKUXBB6H).

## 8. Gaps and opportunities for a couple-quiz brand

1. **No leader for the "couple compatibility test" as a book.** The quiz-book shelf is thin: Summersdale ~155 ratings (UK), KDP clones near zero, LoveBook's quiz book only as used copies. Demand exists for the concept in physical form: Relatable's *Are We Compatible?* shows 15.2K units sold on TikTok Shop alone (2026-11-07) at $20-36, for a product that looks recent (ASIN from late 2025). A scored "how compatible are we / how well do you know each other" book is the natural extension of quiz-couple.com's biggest English page.
2. **Phygital advantage.** Print can't score fairly or keep answers secret; the site can. Each quiz with a QR code to enter answers (or each partner on their own phone, using the site's existing remote mode) and get a compatibility % and a results page: unique vs KDP clones, drives traffic and e-mail capture back to the site, and opens the paid assessment upsell.
3. **Privacy by design within KDP.** The proven pattern is "answer alone, reveal together": duplicate Partner A / Partner B pages (Promptly), two copies (BestSelf), answer grids at the back, plus free printable extra answer sheets on the site for replay (the replay-value complaint about Are We Compatible?).
4. **Price slot $14.99-19.99.** Between $10-13 fill-in books and $40-50 scratch books there is room for a quiz + dates + keepsake hybrid; B&W or standard-colour paperback keeps a $5-6 royalty; avoid colour hardcover on KDP.
5. **Do not fight entrenched titles head-on.** Fill-in "what I love about you" (20K-ratings Knock Knock, 18K Compendium) and 3-year Q&A journals (Potter) are locked; the "Would You Rather for couples" keyword is flooded with 2025-2026 clones. Differentiate on the brand's own format: scored tests, "play online too", series (base / spicy / long-distance / newlyweds / LGBTQ+ inclusive).
6. **Use the site's data to write the book.** Pick the most-played tests and the questions with the best completion, and use real numbers in the copy ("played over N times on quiz-couple.com"). An audience of ~150K visits/month and an e-mail list can produce the first 50-100 reviews that KDP clones never get.
7. **Seasonality.** Valentine's Day 2027 (14 Feb) is the obvious launch target: publishing in early-mid January leaves time for first reviews; then anniversaries, weddings (spring/summer), Christmas.
8. **Long-distance angle** (the site already has remote play): no long-distance-specific leader surfaced in this research (not searched in depth).
