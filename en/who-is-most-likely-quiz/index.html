Why Is the "Who Is the Most" Quiz So Popular?

A perfect game to liven up an evening

The "Who Is the Most?" quiz has become a must-have for parties with friends. Easy to understand, funny and sometimes surprising, it creates unforgettable moments of complicity. The debates around the answers are often the best moments of the game!

Ideal for getting to know each other as friends or a couple

Whether you're a couple, longtime friends or new acquaintances, this quiz reveals how others perceive you. It's a fun way to strengthen bonds and learn more about your group's dynamics.

Questions for every taste

Our database of over 240 "Who Is the Most" questions covers all themes: from classic and family-friendly to spicier ones for adults. Every game is unique thanks to the random selection of 20 questions!

Our Tips for a Successful "Who Is the Most" Quiz

Play honestly

The most fun comes from answering sincerely! Honest answers create the best moments of laughter and complicity.

Discuss together before voting

Take time to talk before each answer. The debates are often funnier than the final result!

Accept results with humor

No hard feelings! The quiz is made for fun. If you're named "the most stubborn", take it with a smile!

FAQ - Frequently Asked Questions

How to play the "Who Is the Most" quiz?
It's very simple! Enter the names of 2 to 8 players, then answer together 20 randomly selected questions from over 240. For each question, vote for the person who best fits. At the end, discover who was most often chosen!
How many questions does the "Who Is the Most" quiz contain?
Our quiz contains over 240 varied "Who Is the Most" questions. Each game randomly selects 20, allowing you to replay many times without getting the same questions!
Is the "Who Is the Most" quiz free?
Yes, the quiz is 100% free and requires no registration. You can play as many times as you want, with different questions each game.
Can you play with more than 2 people?
Absolutely! The "Who Is the Most" quiz is designed for 2 to 8 players. The more you are, the more fun it is! Perfect for parties with friends or family.
Are the "Who Is the Most" questions suitable for all ages?
Our questions range from classic to spicier ones. Most are suitable for everyone, but some naughtier questions are intended for an adult audience. Play with family or friends depending on the desired atmosphere!
How to restart with new questions?
At the end of each game, you have two options: "Restart with different questions" to keep the same players but with 20 new random questions, or "Change players" to start from scratch with new participants.
