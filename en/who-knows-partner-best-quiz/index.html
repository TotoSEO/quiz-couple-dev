What is the "Who Knows Their Partner Best" quiz?

The "Who Knows Best" quiz is an interactive game designed especially for couples who want to test their mutual knowledge in a fun and entertaining way. Inspired by popular TV game shows, this quiz challenges partners to guess each other's preferences, habits, and dreams. If you prefer a more classic format, you can also test your relationship with our 20 compatibility questions.

Unlike classic quizzes where you answer alone, this format involves real interaction between both partners. One guesses, the other validates, then you switch roles. This dynamic is what makes the experience so enriching and revealing.

Tips to get the most out of the quiz

Play without screens between you

Put the phone between you or on a table. The goal is to look into each other's eyes when answering, not stare at a screen.

Elaborate on your answers

Don't just say "yes" or "no". Explain why you think it's the right answer. That's where the real conversations begin.

Don't take the score too seriously

The goal isn't to win, but to have a good time together. Wrong answers are often the funniest and most revealing!

Play regularly

With 100 questions and random draws, each game is different. Make it a monthly ritual to strengthen your connection.

Frequently asked questions about the "Who Knows Best" quiz

How does the "Who Knows the Other Best" quiz work?
Each player takes turns guessing their partner's preferences. The partner then validates if the answer is correct. After 20 questions, scores are compared to see who knows the other best.
How many questions does this couple quiz contain?
Each round includes 20 randomly selected questions from a pool of 100. You can replay as many times as you like with different questions each time.
Is this quiz really free?
Yes, this quiz is 100% free and requires no registration. You can play as many times as you want directly on the site, without creating an account or providing personal information.
What's the purpose of the partner knowledge quiz?
This quiz lets you test your mutual knowledge in a fun way, strengthen your bond, and open dialogue on varied topics: preferences, dreams, fears, daily habits, life projects...
Can you replay with the same questions?
No, each new game draws 20 random questions from the 100 available. This ensures a fresh experience every time and lets you discover new facets of your partner.
Do you need to play at the same time as your partner?
Yes, this quiz is designed to be played by two people simultaneously. You need to be together since one guesses and the other validates the answer. This real-time interaction is what makes the game fun and enriching.
