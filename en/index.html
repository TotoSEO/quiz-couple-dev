What is a couple quiz ?

A couple quiz is a fun questionnaire designed especially for romantic partners. It allows you to evaluate different aspects of your relationship: communication, intimacy, common projects, and much more.

Our quizzes are designed to be fun and constructive . Far from complex psychological tests, they offer you a moment of complicity to share together.

  • Fun and caring questions
  • Suitable for all couples
  • No registration required
  • Immediate and personalized results

What these quizzes are for

Six good reasons to pick up the phone tonight instead of starting another series.

01

Know where you stand

Your strengths, your blind spots, and a score that sums it up. It's more honest than an end-of-evening conversation, and it takes five minutes.

02

Have a proper evening

Nothing to book, nothing to install. One phone, the two of you, and the evening takes a different turn.

03

Learn something new

Even after ten years, there are answers you have never heard. It's often the silliest question that brings them out.

04

Get the spark back

A round where you laugh and tease each other, the way you did at the start. The kind of moment that slips away as the weeks pile up.

05

Say what goes unsaid

Some subjects never come up on their own. A question asked by a screen lands better than a question asked by your partner.

06

Keep something from it

A result to share, a screenshot to send each other, and a conversation that carries on well past the last question.

How it works

Three steps, and you're off.

1

Choose

A serious test or a light game, depending on the mood. Everything is free and nothing asks for an account.

2

Answer

Taking turns on the same phone, or each on your own. The questions come one at a time.

3

Find out

A detailed result, the advice that goes with it, and something to move on to next.
