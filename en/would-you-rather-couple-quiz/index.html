Would You Rather? The couple quiz that will surprise you

100 dilemmas written for couples, 15 or 30 drawn at random each game, whichever you prefer. Funny, occasionally deep, and never the same twice.

The would you rather for couples is one of those rare formats that works every single time. No equipment, no complicated rules, just two people, impossible choices… and answers that say far more than you'd expect.

Three months together or fifteen years, it makes no difference: your partner's answers always have the power to surprise you. That's the whole point of it. This isn't a test with right answers, it's a conversation dressed up as a quiz.

How to play "Would You Rather" as a couple?

The principle is simple, and that's exactly why it works. One question, two options, the other one chooses. No right answer. No wrong one either. Just a choice, and what that choice says about you.

What makes the game genuinely interesting as a couple is that you already know each other. You think you know what they'll say. Then a question lands, the answer you expected doesn't come… and that's when it becomes a conversation.

A few unofficial rules that make games far better: ban “it depends” (it's the answer that kills everything, force the choice even when it's uncomfortable), ask “why?” after every answer (the choice alone says little, the reasoning behind it says plenty), take turns asking, and allow time. A game that starts well can easily run two hours!

What you learn about each other by playing

On paper it's a game. In practice it's one of the rare moments where you ask questions you'd never ask in a normal conversation, and where the other person answers without time to work out what they ought to say.

That's where would you rather starts revealing things as a couple. No dramatic revelations, don't worry. More like small discoveries: a priority you never suspected, a fear surfacing through a trivial choice, a value that comes first when you'd have bet on the other one.

Couples who play this kind of quiz regularly often know the small details about each other better. Not the big life subjects, no: the fine preferences, the everyday trade-offs, the things that matter without ever having been said out loud. It's an excellent companion to get to know your partner better day to day.

"Would You Rather" variations depending on the mood

The basic format is perfect for starting out. But there are variants that completely change the colour of a game.

The "think alike" version

Instead of answering for yourself, you each answer as you think the other one will. Then you compare. The gap between what you imagined and what actually comes out is often revealing… and often very funny.

The speed round version

Five seconds to answer, not one more. It removes all strategic thinking and forces the instinctive answer. This is the variant that surprises people most.

The party version

For playing with several couples: everyone bets on how the others will answer. Those who've known each other for years usually think they'll guess everything. They're wrong far more often than they believe! If you enjoy group games between couples, this variant is made for you.

Questions that make you think (and those that just make you laugh)

There are two categories of question in a good would you rather for couples. Both are useful, but not in the same way.

The light questions are there to put you at ease, to make you laugh, to set a rhythm. They teach you nothing profound, but they create the context that makes the next ones land properly.

The questions that dig open something up. They touch real expectations, needs that haven't always been clearly put into words. The right balance is roughly 60/40: light enough that it stays a game, deep enough that playing was worth it.

Why this quiz works so well for couples

The best questions are the ones where both options genuinely cost something, and where the choice says something true. The format works because it goes around the usual defences: you're not talking about the relationship directly, and yet every answer says a little more about how you see your life together, the everyday, the future.

It's also a format that puts nobody in a losing position. No wrong answers, no score to hit. Just two people discovering, question after question, that they maybe didn't know each other quite as well as they thought. And that's exactly what makes the game worth playing. And if you're after other things to do together as a couple, there are plenty more to find.

FAQ

How many questions does this Would You Rather couple game contain?
The game contains 100 would you rather questions written for couples, each with two options to pick between. When you start, you choose a quick game of 15 dilemmas or a full game of 30, drawn at random, so you can play again without landing on the same ones. And if you enjoyed the first 15, the results screen offers to carry on with the next 15.
Is the Would You Rather game suitable for all couples?
Yes! The format works whatever the couple's age or how long you've been together. The questions are written to make you laugh and think without ever making anyone uncomfortable, and there's nothing to prepare.
Are there any spicy questions in this quiz?
This quiz stays in a fun, reflective register, with no explicitly naughty questions. It covers values, priorities and everyday trade-offs. For something more intimate, take a look at our truth or dare games instead.
Can you play this quiz long-distance?
Absolutely! The format lends itself perfectly to playing at a distance, on video or even by message. Each of you reads the question on your own screen and gives an answer. It's an ideal activity for couples living far apart.
