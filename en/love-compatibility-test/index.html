What is love compatibility?

Love compatibility is how aligned two partners are on the things that shape a life together: values, life plans, money, lifestyle, communication and intimacy. It is not the same as attraction: you can feel strong chemistry while having very different visions of life.

Two compatible people are not clones: they don't need to share everything, but their fundamentals point in the same direction. That alignment, more than early passion, best predicts how solid a relationship will be over time.

Our love compatibility test measures exactly that alignment: you each answer separately, then we compare your answers to work out your compatibility score.

How does our love compatibility test work?

The test is played as a couple. Each of you answers the same 20 questions, in turn, about the big dimensions of a relationship. There are no right or wrong answers, what matters is answering honestly.

At the end, we compare your answers. Each matching answer reveals a point of alignment. Your compatibility score is the percentage of answers where you're on the same wavelength, with a verdict and things to talk about.

The key dimensions of compatibility

Values

Faithfulness, honesty, freedom, family: do your core bearings match?

Life plans

Marriage, children, where to live: are you heading the same way?

Lifestyle

Homebodies or party-goers, pace, holidays: do your habits fit?

Communication

Handling conflict, needing to share everything or not: same language?

Intimacy

Affection, desire, need for closeness: do your expectations meet?

Independence

Togetherness or autonomy, time as two or apart: what balance?

Love compatibility vs chemistry: what's the difference?

Chemistry (attraction) is immediate, physical and emotional, the early spark. Compatibility shows over time, when you have to make decisions, handle everyday life and compromise.

Many strongly attracted couples end up splitting because their fundamentals don't fit. Conversely, solid compatibility helps you weather storms. Ideally you have both, but if you had to bet on the long term, compatibility is the better indicator.

Can you improve your love compatibility?

Partly, yes. Some things are barely negotiable (wanting children or not, for instance), but many differences can be worked on: life pace, money, the way you communicate or handle conflict.

The key isn't to be identical, but to know your differences and talk about them. A mid-range score is not a verdict: it's a map showing where you're aligned and where the conversation deserves to go deeper.

How to read your compatibility score

Your result is a percentage of aligned answers. Here's how to read it:

  • 80% and above, soulmates: your fundamentals are very aligned, an ideal base to build on.
  • 60 to 79%, great compatibility: you meet on the essentials, with a few enriching differences.
  • 40 to 59%, compatibility to nurture: real common ground, but also topics to clarify together.
  • Under 40%, different paths: your views diverge on several fundamentals, dialogue will be valuable.

Remember: a test is no substitute for a real conversation. The best use of your score is to talk it through together.

FAQ: Love Compatibility Test

What is love compatibility?
It's how aligned two partners are on the fundamentals of a life together: values, life plans, money, lifestyle, communication and intimacy. It differs from attraction, which is immediate, whereas compatibility is measured over time.
How is the compatibility score calculated?
You each answer the same 20 questions. The test compares your answers: your compatibility score is the percentage of identical answers, the points where you're aligned.
Do you need two people to take the test?
Yes, it's a couple's test: both partners answer in turn on the same device. It's the comparison of your answers that gives the compatibility score.
How is it different from the names & zodiac compatibility test?
The names & zodiac test is a fun game based on numerology and astrology. This one is a real couple's questionnaire that measures your actual alignment on values, plans and everyday life.
Does a low compatibility score mean we should break up?
No. A low score simply flags differences on several fundamentals. Many of those differences can be worked on through dialogue and compromise. The result is mainly a starting point to talk things through.
Is the test free?
Yes, our love compatibility test is 100% free, with no sign-up or download, and an instant result.
