What this test really reveals

Love compatibility is one of the oldest questions humanity has ever asked. Before dating apps, before personality tests, before psychologists even existed -- there were the stars and numbers. Two systems for reading the world that, each in their own way, seek to understand why some people attract each other like magnets, while others clash without ever truly connecting.

This test brings both together. Not to decide for you. But to offer a different perspective on what is at play between two people.

First name numerology: when letters vibrate

In numerology, each letter of the alphabet carries a number. A equals 1, B equals 2, C equals 3 -- and so on up to 9, then it starts over. This correspondence is one of the oldest in the Western esoteric tradition, dating back to the work of Pythagoras in the 6th century BCE. The idea is simple yet profound -- every first name is a frequency.

To calculate the compatibility between two first names, you add up the values of each letter, reduce the total to a single digit between 1 and 9 (except for the "master numbers" 11 and 22, which retain their own power), and compare. The 1 carries drive and independence. The 2 seeks harmony and union. The 5 craves freedom. Two numbers that complement each other create a balanced couple.

First name numerology does not depend on exact spelling -- it captures the vibration of sounds and letters. If you go by a nickname, try both versions: the results may differ.

Astrology and the signs: the imprint of the sky at birth

Your zodiac sign is not calculated -- it is inherited. It is determined by the position of the Sun at the moment of birth. Twelve signs. Four elements. Fire (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius) -- impulsive, passionate. Earth (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn) -- steady, patient. Air (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius) -- quick, communicative. Water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces) -- deep, intuitive.

Astrological compatibility is first analyzed through these elements. A Fire sign and an Air sign fuel each other. An Earth sign and a Water sign understand each other instinctively. But opposites also attract.

The major affinities between signs

Signs of the same element share a natural understanding. Signs in square create friction -- not necessarily destructive. Signs in opposition embody the paradox of the attraction of opposites: two halves of the same axis.

Why combine both readings?

Astrology speaks of what you received at birth. Numerology speaks of what you carry in your first name. The two readings complement each other because they do not say the same thing.

Your sun sign tells how you love, at a deep level. Your first name number tells how you connect. A Leo whose first name carries the number 2 will be very different from a Leo whose first name carries the number 1. The sign gives the character; the first name shapes how that character unfolds in a relationship.

A high score does not guarantee an easy relationship. A lower score is not a death sentence: it is an invitation to understand where effort will bear fruit.

What these numbers cannot tell you

Neither numerology nor astrology can predict whether a relationship will last. They offer insight. A framework. A way to name what we sometimes feel without being able to explain it.

This test is a starting point, not a verdict. It can open a conversation, confirm an intuition, or simply offer a few minutes of enjoyment.

If you take this test with your partner, the results can be an excellent starting point for a conversation about your differences and complementarities.

Frequently asked questions

Is astrological compatibility really reliable?
Astrology does not predict the future with precision. What it does -- and has done for millennia -- is describe temperaments, ways of being, and modes of relating. That many people find it useful and enlightening is undeniable.
Should I use my full first name or my nickname?
In numerology, the given first name is considered the underlying vibration. The nickname reflects the social vibration. Both are interesting. Try both and see which one resonates with you more.
My sign is "incompatible" with my partner's -- are we doomed?
No. Absolutely not. Compatibility between signs is a tendency, not a rule. It is the people who make relationships -- not the stars.
