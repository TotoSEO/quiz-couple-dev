Pisces love compatibility: love as an ocean

Astrology
Published on March 3, 2026 | Lucie Courtin
  1. Who knows how to love a Pisces
  2. Sign-by-sign compatibility
  3. Discover more

Quick summary

  • Cancer, Scorpio, Taurus: the three partners who understand and protect their sensitivity.
  • With Virgo, their opposite, they form a beautifully complementary couple, intuition meets analysis.
  • Gemini and Sagittarius: two signs too changeable for their need for emotional steadiness.
  • Pisces isn't naive, they choose to believe in the best, even when it's risky.
  • What they seek: someone who protects them without confining them, who loves them without judging.

Pisces is the last sign of the zodiac, and in a way, they carry a piece of every other sign within them. Their sensitivity isn't a weakness. It's an emotional radar that nothing can fool. They perceive what others hide, feel what others refuse to name, and love with an intensity that can be as beautiful as it is destabilizing. To find out whether your sign can navigate those depths, test your love compatibility by sign and name.

Ruled by Neptune, the planet of imagination and dissolved boundaries, Pisces lives love as a total immersion. They don't draw a line between loving someone and merging with that person, which makes them extraordinarily devoted partners, but also vulnerable to unbalanced relationships.

What Pisces looks for in love is someone who understands them without needing everything spelled out. Someone who doesn't mock their sensitivity, who doesn't tell them "you're overreacting" when they cry, and who accepts that love can't be reduced to logic. In return, Pisces offers an empathy and a gentleness few signs can match.

Who knows how to love a Pisces

Cancer (9/10) is their most natural partner. These two Water signs understand each other instinctively, they don't need words to know what the other is feeling. Cancer gives Pisces the emotional security they need, and Pisces offers Cancer a spiritual depth that brings them peace. It's one of the most tender pairings in the zodiac.

Scorpio (9/10) shares with Pisces a rare emotional intensity. These two signs don't do anything halfway, when they commit, it's completely. Scorpio protects Pisces with a fierceness that reassures them, and Pisces softens Scorpio with a kindness that disarms them.

Taurus (8/10) offers Pisces something essential: an anchor. Where Pisces floats, Taurus stands firm. Taurus's solidity reassures Pisces without suffocating them, and Pisces's creativity enchants Taurus without unsettling them.

Virgo (7/10), their zodiac opposite, is a surprising match. Virgo is analytical where Pisces is intuitive. Virgo organizes where Pisces improvises. But that complementarity can create a remarkable balance, Virgo structures Pisces's chaos, and Pisces reminds Virgo that life can't be reduced to lists.

Gemini (4/10) is a fragile compatibility. Gemini shifts opinions, topics, and sometimes moods too quickly for Pisces, who need emotional constancy. Pisces often feels lost in the face of Gemini's lightness, interpreting it as indifference.

Two Pisces together (6/10)? An ocean of tenderness and mutual understanding, but one that can lack structure. Without a minimum of practical grounding, this couple risks drifting together, too absorbed in their emotions to manage everyday life.

Sign-by-sign compatibility

5/10

Pisces + Aries

Pisces (Water) and Aries (Fire) create a surprising combination. The bold Aries and sensitive Pisces can experience profound passion. Pisces leads Aries to explore their emotional dimension, and Aries encourages Pisces to take action. Their differences demand understanding and balance.

8/10

Pisces + Taurus

Pisces (Water) and Taurus (Earth) complement each other well. The empathetic, imaginative Pisces finds stability and comfort in Taurus. The grounded Taurus is touched by Pisces' gentleness and altruism. Their relationship is tender and reassuring.

4/10

Pisces + Gemini

Pisces (Water) and Gemini (Air) don't share the same rhythm. The communicative Gemini may lack depth in the eyes of the dreamy Pisces. Conversely, Pisces may seem too sensitive to the pragmatic Gemini.

9/10

Pisces + Cancer

Pisces (Water) and Cancer (Water) form a highly gentle and empathetic union. Both Water signs share the same sensitivity. The dreamy Pisces finds a protective partner in Cancer, and Cancer receives the emotional understanding they desire from Pisces.

4/10

Pisces + Leo

Pisces (Water) and Leo (Fire) have highly divergent approaches. The flamboyant Leo can crush the introverted Pisces. The overly sensitive Pisces can feel hurt by Leo's frankness.

8/10

Pisces + Virgo

Pisces (Water) and Virgo (Earth) are opposites, but this creates beautiful complementarity. The grounded Virgo organizes Pisces' life, who in turn brings inspiration and tenderness to Virgo. They balance each other out wonderfully.

4/10

Pisces + Libra

Pisces (Water) and Libra (Air) have different expectations. The romantic, emotional Pisces can feel misunderstood by the cerebral Libra. The pragmatic Libra may find Pisces too indecisive.

8/10

Pisces + Scorpio

Pisces (Water) and Scorpio (Water) complement each other incredibly well. They share deep emotional understanding. The intense Scorpio protects Pisces' dreams, and Pisces soothes Scorpio's intensity.

3/10

Pisces + Sagittarius

Pisces (Water) and Sagittarius (Fire) are very different. The enthusiastic Sagittarius may lack sensitivity in Pisces' eyes, and Pisces may seem too soft to Sagittarius.

8/10

Pisces + Capricorn

Pisces (Water) and Capricorn (Earth) match well. The structured Capricorn provides the solid foundation Pisces craves, and Pisces brings compassion and creativity to Capricorn. They form a balanced couple.

2/10

Pisces + Aquarius

Pisces (Water) and Aquarius (Air) struggle to understand each other. The independent, rational Aquarius can seem insensitive to Pisces, and Pisces seems too intuitive for Aquarius. They lack common elements without major compromises.

8/10

Pisces + Pisces

Enormous emotional complicity and shared imagination. Two Pisces easily merge into the world of dreams and creativity, though they risk disconnecting from practical reality.
