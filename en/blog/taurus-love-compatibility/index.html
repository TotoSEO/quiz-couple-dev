Taurus love compatibility: what the stars say about their love stories

Astrology
Published on March 3, 2026
  1. The unions that last, and the ones that stall
  2. Sign-by-sign compatibility
  3. Discover more

Quick summary

  • Taurus with Taurus, Virgo, or Capricorn: some of the most enduring unions in the zodiac. Sometimes a little too safe.
  • With Cancer, it's velvet, two signs that want exactly the same things.
  • Gemini, Aquarius, Sagittarius: three pairings that often start on a misunderstanding.
  • Taurus doesn't forgive betrayal easily. Once trust is broken, it's rarely rebuilt.
  • Their real risk in love: mistaking stability for stagnation.

Ask a Taurus what they want in love. They'll tell you, maybe not in these exact words, something that sounds like consistency. Not boring routine, not mechanical repetition. But the certainty that tomorrow, the other person will still be there. That what was built together won't vanish at the first storm. If you want to see whether your sign-and-name combination lives up to that promise, try our zodiac and name compatibility tool.

Venus rules this sign. And contrary to what you might expect, Venus in Taurus isn't primarily about romance and grand declarations, it's about tangible pleasure, physical presence, accessible beauty. A good meal shared. A comfortable sofa. The warmth of someone you truly know. That's what Taurus calls love.

This search for solidity isn't fear. It's a choice. And the signs that understand that, who don't need the relationship to be constantly reinventing itself to feel alive, are the ones Taurus genuinely thrives with.

The unions that last, and the ones that stall

Two Tauruses together (9/10) is the promise of a stable home and absolute loyalty. The risk? That neither one ever takes the initiative to change anything, even when change is needed. Their combined stubbornness can make disagreements painfully slow to resolve.

With Virgo and Capricorn (9/10 each), Taurus finds partners who share their core values: hard work, reliability, building for the long term. These unions may lack the cinematic romance of a Hollywood love story, but they hold.

On the opposite end, Gemini (3/10), Aquarius (3/10), and Sagittarius (3/10) represent pairings that often start from genuine attraction but quickly run into fundamentally incompatible needs. Taurus wants to put down roots. These three signs want, to varying degrees, the freedom to keep moving. That's not a judgment, it's simply a mismatch in rhythm that demands enormous effort to overcome.

Cancer (9/10) deserves special mention: it's perhaps the most naturally harmonious match for Taurus. Both signs are looking for the same thing, a warm home, a love that protects, and they find each other without having to force it. If you're a Taurus-Cancer pair, there's a good chance you've already noticed how effortlessly things flow between you.

Sign-by-sign compatibility

5/10

Taurus + Aries

Taurus (Earth, Venus) and Aries (Fire, Mars) have highly contrasting temperaments. Taurus loves stability and simple pleasures; Aries seeks immediate action. This elemental opposition creates daily friction: Taurus feels rushed by Aries' impulsiveness, and Aries feels stifled by Taurus' leisurely pace. However, they can find balance: Aries is seduced by Taurus' constancy, and Taurus by Aries' fire. Their relationship relies heavily on compromise and mutual respect.

9/10

Taurus + Taurus

Two Tauruses together (Fixed Earth, ruled by Venus) share great affinity. They both love material comfort, gentleness, and loyalty. The Taurus-Taurus couple builds a solid home: each understands the other's needs (financial security, physical affection, stability). Their mutual stubbornness can cause some conflicts, but their patience often makes up for this refusal to yield. Ultimately, they find comfort in each other, forming a very stable and lasting bond.

3/10

Taurus + Gemini

Taurus (Earth) and Gemini (Air) have opposing needs. Taurus is fixated on security and routine; Gemini seeks diversity and movement. Their compatibility is low: their communication styles (pragmatic Taurus vs. chatty Gemini) differ radically. Taurus appreciates deep, reliable exchanges, while Gemini bounces between topics, which can irritate Taurus. They must be ready to understand each other despite these stark differences.

9/10

Taurus + Cancer

Taurus (Earth) and Cancer (Water) form a harmonious and solid couple. Their harmony is based on a shared goal of security and comfort. The pragmatic Taurus reassures the faithful Cancer, while the highly affectionate Cancer feels well-supported by Taurus. They build a warm home together. Only their difference in expressing emotions (Cancer needs reassuring words, Taurus prefers concrete actions) might require mutual adjustments.

4/10

Taurus + Leo

Taurus (Earth) and Leo (Fire) often have opposing values. The grounded Taurus loves tranquility and security; the flamboyant Leo seeks admiration and luxury. Initially, a strong attraction may exist (Taurus admires Leo's presence and vice versa), but their differences quickly surface. Taurus may feel jostled by Leo's exuberance, and Leo might judge Taurus as too modest. Their relationship requires major efforts to balance Taurus' stability with Leo's passion.

9/10

Taurus + Virgo

Taurus (Earth) and Virgo (Earth) are both highly pragmatic. Their elemental affinity ensures they understand each other perfectly. They share the same values: a sense of the concrete, loyalty, and a sense of responsibility. Taurus brings comfort and constancy to Virgo, while Virgo brings rigor and attention to detail to Taurus. Together, they form a very harmonious couple, living in stability and foresight.

5/10

Taurus + Libra

Taurus (Earth) and Libra (Air) are both ruled by Venus but differ greatly. Taurus seeks material and emotional security; Libra seeks relational balance and aesthetic pleasures. They can bond over a shared sense of elegance and stable relationships, but Taurus often finds Libra indecisive, and Libra judges Taurus as too materialistic. This combination requires patience: Libra brings gentleness, Taurus brings loyalty, but they must work to reconcile Earth and Air.

8/10

Taurus + Scorpio

Taurus (Earth) and Scorpio (Water) are opposites in the zodiac, generating a strong magnetic attraction. The stable Taurus is seduced by Scorpio's emotional depth, and Scorpio is drawn to Taurus' reliability. Together, they form a powerful balance: emotional security for Scorpio, shared passion for Taurus. Their relationship is marked by mutual loyalty and commitment. Intimacy is intense (a shared taste for sensuality), but their respective stubbornness requires good listening skills to maintain harmony.

3/10

Taurus + Sagittarius

Taurus (Earth) and Sagittarius (Fire) have very little in common. The rigorous Taurus loves habits, while the adventurous Sagittarius constantly seeks new experiences. Their elements clash (Earth vs. Fire), making complicity difficult. Taurus may reproach Sagittarius for being unpredictable, and Sagittarius finds Taurus too conservative. Their relationship demands huge concessions or risks running out of steam quickly.

9/10

Taurus + Capricorn

Taurus (Earth) and Capricorn (Earth) share strong elemental compatibility. Both are hardworking, loyal, and pragmatic. Taurus appreciates the financial stability Capricorn aims for, and Capricorn admires Taurus' constancy. Together, they build a solid life: they plan the future (home, career) jointly. Their relationship is highly reliable thanks to this shared goal of material security.

3/10

Taurus + Aquarius

Taurus (Earth) and Aquarius (Air) have highly divergent needs. Taurus seeks routine and security; Aquarius loves the unconventional and independence. These fundamental differences complicate their harmony: Taurus may find Aquarius distant, and Aquarius may find Taurus possessive. Their relationship requires significant compromise to work.

8/10

Taurus + Pisces

Taurus (Earth) and Pisces (Water) complement each other well. The solid Taurus reassures the sensitive Pisces, who in turn brings poetry and tenderness to Taurus. They share a deep love for comfort and gentleness: the relationship is often very affectionate. Taurus stabilizes Pisces' dreams, and Pisces expands Taurus' imagination. Together, they can build a highly harmonious emotional cocoon.
