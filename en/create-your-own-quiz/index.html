Build an online quiz in minutes

Our free quiz maker lets you build your own questionnaire in a few clicks, with no sign-up. Pick a template (points quiz, true or false, or just for fun), add up to 30 questions with a maximum of 4 answers each, and you're done.

Perfect for testing your friends, challenging your partner or hosting a game night: anyone can play straight from their phone or computer.

Private or public quiz: your choice

By default your quiz is private: it can only be opened with the share link generated on creation. Keep that link safe, because it's the only way to find your quiz again. Private quizzes are kept for one week.

You can also make your quiz public: it then appears in the community quiz list on this page, and anyone can play it. Public quizzes are kept.
