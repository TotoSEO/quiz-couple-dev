Your questions

What photos go in the book?

Each page has a frame the size of a mini instant photo. A printed photo cut to size works too. A glue stick or double-sided tape holds better than liquid glue, which makes the paper wrinkle.

Do we have to do the adventures in order?

No. Open a page at random or pick one from the list, depending on your budget, your time and the season.

How long does it take to fill?

There’s no deadline. Some couples will fill it in a year, others in ten. The book stays with you.

Why “Pink” and “Purple”?

They’re the two Quiz Couple characters. At the start of the book, you each pick yours and keep its color until the last page. No “his” and “hers”: it works for every couple.

Will it arrive before Christmas?

It’s printed and shipped by Amazon. The delivery date shows on its page before you order.

Can I order it outside the US?

The button opens Amazon.com. If you live in the UK, Canada or Australia, your local Amazon store may have it too: search for “100 Adventures for Two”.
