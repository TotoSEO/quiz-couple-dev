Privacy Policy

This privacy policy defines and informs you of how Quiz Couple uses and protects the information you provide to us, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Data Controller

  • Name: Thomas Owaller
  • Email: service@quiz-couple.com
  • Website: quiz-couple.com

2. Data Collected

2.1 Local Storage

To improve your experience, we use your browser's local storage (LocalStorage) for:

  • Saving your current quiz progress
  • Remembering your theme preferences (light or dark mode)
  • Keeping results from your previous quizzes

Important: This data is stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or to third parties. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's browsing data.

2.2 IP address and abuse prevention

Some features are open without an account. To stop the same person leaving ten reviews on the same test or voting several times on the same dilemma, we process one technical item:

  • IP address: used only to recognise a submission already made and block duplicates
  • Date of submission: to apply that limit over time

Important: This data is not used for profiling, advertising or audience measurement. It is never cross-referenced with the answers you give in the quizzes.

2.3 Data collected via download forms

When you request free content (e-book, guide), we collect the following information with your explicit consent:

  • First name: To personalise our communications
  • Email address: To send you the requested content and, with your consent, occasional informational communications
  • Request subject: To identify the content you requested

Important: You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us at the address indicated below. Your data will then be deleted within 30 days.

2.4 Site audience measurement

To know which pages are read and which are not, we count visits ourselves, without going through an outside service. Each page displayed records three pieces of information, and nothing else:

  • The page address: to know which one was viewed, and in which language
  • A randomly drawn visit number: to link together the pages of a single visit. It disappears after 30 minutes without a new page and is never reused: if you come back tomorrow you are a new visit, and nothing makes it possible to connect it to the previous one
  • The site you came from: its domain name only (a search engine, a social network), never the full address nor the words you typed

Important: No IP address, no browser fingerprint and no personal data is recorded. These figures serve only to produce overall statistics for this site, are shared with no one and are cross-referenced with no other source. On that basis this measurement falls under the consent exemption for audience measurement and therefore does not depend on the cookie banner. The data is kept for 13 months at most.

3. Cookies, analytics and advertising

Quiz Couple uses Google Analytics 4 to measure site traffic, and Google AdSense to display ads. Both services place cookies in your browser.

  • Both services follow Google Consent Mode v2: no analytics or advertising cookie is placed before you give explicit consent through our banner
  • If you decline, Google collects no browsing data and any ads shown are not personalised. Our own audience measurement, described in section 2.4, keeps running: it uses neither cookies nor personal data
  • Analytics data is anonymised and cannot be used to identify you personally
  • If you accept, Google AdSense may use your browsing to personalise ads. You can change this at any time from Google ad settings

You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser cookies. For more information on Google's cookie management, see Google's privacy policy.

4. Security

We implement the following security measures to protect your browsing:

  • HTTPS connection: All communications between your browser and our site are encrypted
  • Secure database: Technical data is stored securely with strict access policies
  • Data minimization: We only collect data strictly necessary for the service to function
  • Secure source code: We follow best practices in secure web development

5. Your Rights

In accordance with the GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: Obtain confirmation of whether your data is being processed
  • Right of rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Right to portability: Receive your data in a structured format
  • Right to object: Object to the processing of your data

To exercise these rights regarding usage limitation data, contact us at the address below.

6. Data hosting and processors

The site itself is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, United States). The data you send us is stored in a database managed by Supabase, acting as a processor under the GDPR. This covers the first name and comment you leave in a review, messages sent through the contact form, the email address and first name provided when requesting free content, and the anti-duplicate technical data described in 2.2. Quiz plays are counted in a form that cannot identify you: only the test name and the language are recorded, never your answers. None of these processors is allowed to use this data for its own purposes.

7. Links to External Sites

Our site may contain links to external sites. We invite you to consult the privacy policies of these sites, as we are not responsible for their data protection practices.

8. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this privacy policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an update date. We encourage you to check this page regularly.

9. Contact

For any question or request for information about the site, please use our contact form.
