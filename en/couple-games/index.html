What a game for two changes about an evening

A couple game looks like nothing at all. And yet it solves a very concrete problem: as a pair, you always end up circling the same subjects. The day, work, what's for dinner… A game imposes a frame, and that frame gives you permission to ask questions you'd never ask out of nowhere.

It's also why a game works better than a film when you genuinely want to reconnect. In front of a screen you're two people side by side looking elsewhere. Around a game you're two people looking at each other. You feel the difference within ten minutes.

The eight games on this page run online, free, on one phone. Nothing to create, nothing to install, nothing to prepare.

That's the real question. And the answer depends far less on your tastes than on the moment you happen to be in.

Making a games night for two work

Four things separate a game you abandon after ten minutes from an evening you're still talking about the next day.

1

One phone, set down between you

Two screens means two attentions elsewhere. Our games are built to run on one device that you pass back and forth, and it completely changes the mood.

2

Airplane mode, seriously

A single notification is enough to break a moment that was just getting interesting. It's the most obvious tip on this list… and by far the most effective.

3

The right to pass

A game for two only works if nobody feels cornered. Agree from the start that either of you can refuse a card without explaining: both card games have a forfeit for exactly that.

4

Start softer than you planned

The classic mistake is opening with the boldest game. Start light: the rest follows on its own once the laughing has started.

Frequently asked questions

Are these couple games really free?

Yes, all seven, from the first to the last. No account to create, no paid tier, no content unlocked after payment. Open the game's page and play.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Everything runs straight in your browser, on a phone or a computer. That's also what lets you start a game in ten seconds, right when the urge strikes.

What is the best game to play as a couple at home?

It depends on the evening! Truth or dare is the most versatile: it starts fast and stops whenever you want. Which one of us fits in five minutes and starts a conversation every single time. Would you rather works better when you feel like talking more than doing. The board game needs a proper half hour, but it's the only one you can win. The wheel is mostly for settling a bet. And the naughty version is for the moments when it's just the two of you.

Can we play remotely, each at home?

Yes. One of you opens the game, reads out loud on video and confirms for both. Truths, dilemmas and votes work perfectly at a distance, dares take a bit of imagination.

Are these games suitable for a couple who have just met?

Would you rather, which one of us, the forfeit wheel and the classic truth or dare, yes: none of them assume a shared history. The board game too, though its memory squares will feel a little thin early on. The naughty version, though, assumes you already know each other well: played too early it makes people more uncomfortable than amused.

A game repairs nothing, but it opens doors

Let's be honest about what a game can do. It doesn't settle a fundamental disagreement, and it doesn't replace a conversation when one is needed. What it does very well is strip out the solemnity: it's the card asking the question, not you. That small shift is often enough to unlock a subject.
