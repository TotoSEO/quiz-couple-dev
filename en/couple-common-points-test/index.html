Couple Common Points Test: Are You Really Compatible?

Discover your affinities, shared tastes, and common values with this interactive test. 20 random questions from 200 for a unique experience every time!

This common points test is designed for couples who want to know each other better, discover their affinities, and measure their compatibility in a fun and interactive way.

Why common points matter in a couple

Common points form the foundation of a fulfilling relationship. Sharing tastes, values, or a vision of life creates a solid basis for complicity and mutual understanding. When two people agree on important subjects, communication becomes smoother and conflicts less frequent.

However, common points aren't everything. Differences bring richness and complementarity. The key is finding the balance between what unites you and what makes you unique.

What is a couple's common points test for?

This test allows you to know each other better by exploring varied themes: your tastes, habits, values, and plans. Each question is an opportunity to exchange and discover sometimes unsuspected facets of your partner.

Beyond the final score, it's a tool to open dialogue. Why did you answer that? What made you choose this option? These discussions enrich your relationship and strengthen your connection.

How to interpret the test results

The final score gives you an indication of your common points, but it doesn't define the quality of your relationship. A high score shows beautiful harmony, while a lower score reveals differences that can be sources of richness.

The key is to step back and use this test as a starting point for constructive discussions. Each difference is an opportunity to better understand the other.

Do you need many common points to be compatible?

Not necessarily! Compatibility relies on much more than the number of common points. The strongest couples are often those who know how to balance similarities and differences.

The important thing is to share fundamental values (family, fidelity, life projects) while accepting that each person has their own tastes and personality. Differences can bring complementarity and surprise to the relationship.
